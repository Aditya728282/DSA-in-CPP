        Pet Animals Name and Their Benefits : 
        Goat: – Goat’s milk is very beneficial, sometimes even in serious illnesses, doctors advise consuming goat’s milk, let us tell you that there are more than 300 species of goat in the whole world. Goat lives only 10 to 15 years.

        Dog :  – The dog is the most loyal pet in the world, it has always been a good friend of humans, often the dog is kept for the protection of people and their home.

        cow : – Cow’s milk contains protein, cow is beneficial for us in every way, cow’s urine, milk, and dung all things are useful for humans.

        Buffalo:-  Buffalo is kept by people for milk.

        Horse:  – Horses are reared by people for their hobby, such horses have also been helping a lot the early man, they have also been good companions of human beings.

        Sheep:- Sheep are used to extract wool from them.

        Donkey: – These animals work very hard, they are used for carrying loads.

        Camel: – Camel is called the ship of the desert, its age is about 50 years, this animal can run in the desert at a speed of about 65 kilometers per hour, and the camel can live without drinking water for two months.

        cat: – Cats are raised by people as a hobby and it sleeps for about 13 to 15 hours.
